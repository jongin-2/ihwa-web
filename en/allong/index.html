About Allong

Allong is a walking adventure app built around a small creature that hatches from an egg. Your steps help it grow, and when you walk a real map you meet materials and Dallongs that look like the places they come from. Instead of a bare step counter, your route becomes the adventure, and your Allong also shows up on Home Screen widgets and the Lock Screen. It is free with no purchases, and coming soon.

FAQ

What is Allong?

Allong is an iPhone walking app where you explore a real map with a small creature that grows as you walk. Along the way you collect materials and meet Dallongs.

When is Allong coming out?

It is coming soon. The exact date is not set yet.

Is Allong free?

Yes, it is free to play and has no purchases.

How are steps counted?

Steps are counted from the distance you actually move, so treadmill steps do not count. Turn on "Walk with the app closed" in Settings to keep counting while the app is closed.

What is a Dallong?

A Dallong is born when moonlight lights up a real creature or object, so it looks like the place it lives. Win and you get special materials; lose and you lose nothing.

Can I see it on widgets and the Lock Screen?

Yes. Home Screen widgets come in small, medium and large, and there are Lock Screen widgets and a Live Activity while you explore.

Do I need an account?

No, you can start right away as a guest. Link an account to keep your Allong when you switch devices.

Who can use it, and what shows in rankings?

You must be 14 or older. In the weekly rankings, your Allong's name and records are visible to other players.
