About DrinkChecker

Somewhere around the third round, most of us lose count of how many we've had. With DrinkChecker you set your own limit first, then tap the widget each time you finish a glass. The ring fills up as you go, so you can see where you stand tonight, and if you pass your line it tells you once. There's no sign-up, you just open it and start.

FAQ

Is DrinkChecker free?

Yes, it is free, and every feature works without signing in.

Is there an iPhone app to log how much I drink?

DrinkChecker is one. You pick a drink such as soju, beer, wine or sake, log it glass by glass, and review past entries on a calendar and trend chart.

Can I log a drink without opening the app?

Yes. Pick a drink and tap on the Home Screen widget and it is logged. During a session you can also add a drink, undo, or switch drinks from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

How do I set my drinking limit?

Choose your usual drink and enter how many glasses feel right for you. The app does not set a recommended amount for you.

What happens when I go over my limit?

You get one calm notification, with no guilt-tripping warning.

Where are my records stored?

Without an account they stay on your device only. You can connect an Apple or Google account if you want a backup that carries over to a new phone.

Does it work on iPad?

On iPad it installs in iPhone-app compatibility mode. There is no Mac app and no Android version yet.

Does DrinkChecker give medical advice or recommend how much to drink?

No. It does not provide medical advice or recommended amounts. It is a tool for recording and seeing the limit you chose, intended for people of legal drinking age.
